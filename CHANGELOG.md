# Changelog

## 0.5.0

First public source release.

- Native translucent menu bar panel with aligned controls.
- Independent mouse/touch and vertical/horizontal reversal settings.
- Permission-gated master switch and recovery after sleep/event-tap interruptions.
- Dedicated Settings page with English, Turkish, German and French.
- Persistent preferences and optional launch at login.
- Local build script, deterministic checks and MIT license.

Current distribution targets Apple Silicon on macOS 13 or later. Local builds are ad-hoc signed and are not notarized. Physical-device classification remains heuristic.
